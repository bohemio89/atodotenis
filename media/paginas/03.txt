Skupski salutes outgoing captain Smith, previews semi-final
26 Nov 26
David Kane
SHARE
FACEBOOK
X
EMAIL

Fresh off an emotional 2-1 victory over Germany, Great Britain’s Neal Skupski revealed captain Leon Smith’s decision to step down after 2026 has provided extra motivation at the Davis Cup by Generali Final 8 in Bologna.

“I think all week there's been a different type of energy in the squad,” Skupski said after he and new partner Henry Patten defeated Kevin Krawietz and Tim Puetz in the deciding doubles match. “I think we obviously are here for Team GB, but we know the situation with Leon. Yeah, everyone's come together. We want to do well for Leon in his last Davis Cup ties. It's amazing that we can have another match on Saturday.

“I think at the back of your mind you think fight for every ball, because what Leon's given to Team GB, to British tennis over the years, it's incredible.”

Smith took the reins as captain in 2010 and led Great Britain to the title in 2015, their first since 1936. After winning two 4-0 ties in qualifying, Team GB is now into their first semi-final in seven years, guaranteeing Smith at least 45 ties.

“He's amazing on the bench,” continued Skupski. “He makes you very relaxed when you are feeling a lot of pressure. Even like today, some words of wisdom. Yeah, it's very nice.

Yeah, we don't really want it to end.

“Maybe you want this question to end, but…” he laughingly added to a blushing Smith.

“Whatever happens, whatever team we play, we are going to give it all for Team GB, but a little bit extra for Leon because it's amazing.”

Great Britain will face either Austria or Spain for a place in the final, and neither Smith nor Skupski wanted to take it for granted that they would take on a Carlos Alcaraz-led Spain on Saturday.

“Last night it was a flip of the coin,” Smith said, citing Italy’s narrow victory over Korea, Rep. “Could have gone either way.”

“Obviously they've got to play Austria tonight,” Skupski agreed. “They are no easy feat. They've got a great doubles team as well in Miedler and Oberleitner, who won Shanghai. They would be very difficult to beat if they get on the court tonight.

“We have to be prepared for whoever we play against. We'll watch the match tonight and study some stuff tomorrow, ready for the match on Saturday.”

Great Britain will play the second semifinal on Saturday, November 28 at 12:00 CET.
