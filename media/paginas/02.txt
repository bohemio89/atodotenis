Merida scores opening win for Spain
26 Nov 26
David Kane
SHARE
FACEBOOK
X
EMAIL

The last quarter-final tie of the 2026 Davis Cup by Generali Final 8 is underway, and Spain has taken the early lead over Austria on Saturday.

Daniel Merida played the key points better to ease past Joel Schwaerzler, 6-4, 6-4, scoring key breaks in each set to put Spain in pole position for a second straight semi-final.

“The conditions here for me are a bit tough, a little too fast for me!” Merida said on court after the match. “But I was trying to play as much balls in as possible to let him play a bit because he was missing a bit. That was my tactic for today, and I think I did a great job.”

Schwaerzler acquitted himself well in his first Davis Cup match, saving three match points from 0-40 down, but Merida steadied when the match was on his racquet and served his way to victory after one hour and 18 minutes.

At 22 years old, Merida is playing his first Davis Cup season, debuting alongside Rafael Jodar to lead Spain to victory against Chile in September. Jodar is set to follow Merida on court for the second singles match against Jurij Rodionov.

Carlos Alcaraz arrived in Bologna fresh off his first Nitto ATP Finals title, but the former world No. 1 will only play if the tie heads to a deciding doubles match.

“Carlos wants to play but he finished three or four days ago with some pain,” Merida explained, “so he was sitting there, cheering for me. I was a bit nervous because I knew that if we do a great job today, he’s going to play on Saturday, so that was my job for today.”

Jodar was also in Turin to cap off a breakthrough season, and the 20-year-old is eager to end his year on the highest possible note.

“Representing my country means a lot to me,” Jodar said earlier this week. “It’s something I dreamed about when I was a kid. It’s something unique and really looking forward to starting my Davis Cup Final 8 for the first time. I think we have a strong team. My teammates and all the people involved in this Spanish team is supporting me a lot. They know it’s not easy for me because I played a lot of matches in the first year. At the same time, I have energy and I have a lot of ambition to achieve this thing.”

Austria owns an intriguing 3-2 head-to-head against Spain, but their last win over the six-time Davis Cup champions came in 1995. Still, Jurij Rodionov has been a clutch member of the squad, winning the deciding matches in both of Austria’s qualifying ties this season.

The winner will face Great Britain, who knocked out an Alexander Zverev-led Germany, 2-1, earlier in the day.

TIE INFORMATION

Venue: BolognaFiere, Bologna, Italy (hard, indoors)

Austria (No. 6): Jurij Rodionov, Lukas Neumayer, Joel Schwaerzler, Neil Oberleitner, Lucas Miedler, Jurgen Melzer (captain)

Spain (No. 2): Carlos Alcaraz, Rafael Jodar, Daniel Merida, Pedro Martinez, Marcel Granollers, David Ferrer (captain)

THURSDAY, NOVEMBER 24 (START @ 17:15 CET)

Daniel Merida (ESP) d Joel Schwaerzler (AUT) 6-4 6-4

Jurij Rodionov (AUT) v Rafael Jodar (ESP)

Lucas Miedler/Neil Oberleitner (AUT) v Carlos Alcaraz/Marcel Granollers (ESP)

HEAD-TO-HEAD

Austria leads Spain, 3-2.
